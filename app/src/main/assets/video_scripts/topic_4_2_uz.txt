4.2 — Ehtimollarni qo‘shish va ko‘paytirish. Shartli ehtimol, to‘la ehtimol va Beyes
1. Birgalikda bo‘lmagan hodisalarda P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B).
2. Umumiy formula P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B).
3. Mustaqillik: P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B).
4. Shartli ehtimol P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B).
5. To‘la ehtimol formulasi ΣP(H_i)P(A|H_i).
6. Beyes: P(H_i|A)=P(H_i)P(A|H_i)/P(A).
Video ichki savol: P(A)=0.9 va P(B)=0.8 mustaqil bo‘lsa kamida bittasi yuz berish ehtimolini toping.
