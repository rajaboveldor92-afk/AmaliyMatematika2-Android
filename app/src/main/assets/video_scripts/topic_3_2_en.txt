APPLIED MATHEMATICS 2 — 3.2
Separable differential equations

1. Start from f1(x)φ1(y)dx+f2(x)φ2(y)dy=0.
2. Group x-dependent terms with dx and y-dependent terms with dy.
3. Integrate both sides and introduce C.
4. For x(y²−4)dx+y dy=0: y²−4=Ce^(−x²).
5. For y′=tan x·tan y: sin y cos x=C.
6. An initial condition determines C.

CHECKPOINT: Separate y′=2xy. Answer: dy/y=2x dx.
