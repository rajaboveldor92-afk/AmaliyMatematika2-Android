APPLIED MATHEMATICS 2 — 3.4
Linear equations with constant coefficients

1. Build the characteristic equation by replacing derivatives with powers of k.
2. Distinct real roots: C1e^(k1x)+C2e^(k2x).
3. Repeated root: (C1+C2x+...)e^(kx).
4. Roots α±βi: e^(αx)(C1cosβx+C2sinβx).
5. Initial/boundary conditions determine the constants.
6. Nonhomogeneous general solution = homogeneous general solution + a particular solution.
7. In resonance, multiply the trial form by a suitable power of x.
