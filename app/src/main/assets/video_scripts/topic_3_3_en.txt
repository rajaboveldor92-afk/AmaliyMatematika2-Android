APPLIED MATHEMATICS 2 — 3.3
Homogeneous, linear and Bernoulli equations

1. f(λx,λy)=λ^m f(x,y): homogeneous of degree m.
2. Use y=tx, dy=xdt+t dx for homogeneous first-order equations.
3. Linear equation: y′+P(x)y=Q(x).
4. Homogeneous linear solution: y=Ce^(−∫Pdx).
5. Variation of constants: C→C(x).
6. Bernoulli: y′+Py=Qy^n, z=y^(1−n).
7. For y′+xy=xy³: z=y⁻².

CHECKPOINT: If n=4, what exponent appears in z? 1−n=−3.
