APPLIED MATHEMATICS 2 — 3.1
Basic concepts of differential equations

1. A differential equation relates an unknown function and its derivatives.
2. An ordinary differential equation has one independent variable.
3. The order is the highest derivative order.
4. First order: F(x,y,y′)=0 or y′=f(x,y).
5. General solution y=φ(x,C); a particular solution fixes C=C0.
6. Cauchy problem: y′=f(x,y), y(x0)=y0.
7. Integral curves and uniqueness.

CHECKPOINT: What is the order of xy′+sin(x)y′′=0? Answer: 2.
