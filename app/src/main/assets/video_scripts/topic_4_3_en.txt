4.3 — Bernoulli scheme, Poisson and De Moivre–Laplace
1. Independent two-outcome trials with constant success probability p.
2. P_n(k)=C_n^k p^k q^(n-k), q=1−p.
3. Most probable count: np−q≤k_0≤np+p.
4. Poisson approximation: λ=np, P≈e^(−λ)λ^k/k!.
5. Local De Moivre–Laplace and x=(k−np)/sqrt(npq).
6. Integral approximation for k_1≤k≤k_2.
