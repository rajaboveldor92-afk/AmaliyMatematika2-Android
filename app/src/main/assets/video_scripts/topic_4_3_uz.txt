4.3 — Bernulli sxemasi, Puasson va Muavr–Laplas
1. Ikki natijali, mustaqil, bir xil p ehtimolli sinovlar.
2. P_n(k)=C_n^k p^k q^(n-k), q=1−p.
3. Eng ehtimolli son np−q≤k_0≤np+p.
4. Puasson taqribi: λ=np, P≈e^(−λ)λ^k/k!.
5. Muavr–Laplas lokal formula va standartlashtirish x=(k−np)/sqrt(npq).
6. Integral formula: k_1≤k≤k_2 ehtimoli.
