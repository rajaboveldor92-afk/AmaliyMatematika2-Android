4.6 — Expectation, variance and standard deviation
1. Discrete M[X]=Σx_i p_i.
2. D[X]=M[(X−M[X])²]=M[X²]−M[X]².
3. σ=sqrt(D[X]).
4. Binomial: M=np, D=npq.
5. Poisson: M=D=λ.
6. Continuous: M[X]=∫xf(x)dx.
7. Mode maximizes density; median splits probability in half.
