4.2 — Addition and multiplication of probabilities. Conditional/total probability and Bayes
1. Mutually exclusive: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B).
2. General rule: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B).
3. Independence: P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B).
4. Conditional probability P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B).
5. Total probability ΣP(H_i)P(A|H_i).
6. Bayes: P(H_i|A)=P(H_i)P(A|H_i)/P(A).
