APPLIED MATHEMATICS 2 — 1.1 DEFINITE INTEGRAL
Language: English

1. Riemann sum
Partition [a,b] and form S_n=Σf(ξ_i)Δx_i.

Interactive pause:
What is the mesh of a partition?
Answer: max Δx_i.

2. Definition
If S_n has a partition-independent limit as max Δx_i→0, that limit is ∫_a^b f(x)dx.

3. Newton–Leibniz formula
If F'=f:
∫_a^b f(x)dx=F(b)-F(a).

4. Riemann-sum example
∫_0^1 x²dx=1/3.
The screen displays Riemann rectangles for n from 2 to 30 and the approximation error.

5. Substitution
x=φ(t), dx=φ'(t)dt, and the integration limits are transformed too.

6. Integration by parts
∫u dv=uv-∫v du.
Example: ∫_0^π(x-π)cos x dx=-2.
